Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026.

Who uses it. Seb Workspace is a private tool used only by its owner, on his own computer. There are no other users.

What it reads. With the owner's permission it reads his own Google Calendar: the list of his calendars and their events. It uses the read-only scopes calendar.calendarlist.readonly and calendar.events.readonly. It never adds, changes or deletes anything.

Where the data goes. The calendar data is shown in the owner's workspace and stored only on his own computer. It is not sold, shared, sent to third parties, or used for advertising. It is not used to train AI models.

Keys. The sign-in keys are kept in the computer's own keychain.

Removing access. The owner can remove access at any time in his Google Account under Security, Third-party connections. Removing it stops all reading.

Seb Workspace's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
