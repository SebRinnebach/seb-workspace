Arrow

Arrow helps you stop feeling behind.

Arrow is a calm workspace that runs on your own Mac. Its Chief of Staff, an AI assistant, helps you plan your day, keep your projects together and remember what matters.

Arrow is in testing. Only people invited by its maker use it.

If you connect your Google Calendar, Arrow reads your calendars and their events (read-only) so your day appears in Arrow. It cannot add, change or delete anything.
